KEY FEATURES IMPLEMENTED:
1. Newspaper Card Flip:
   - Click to open/close like a real newspaper
   - Front shows invitation, back reveals heartfelt message
   - Smooth 3D flip animation with perspective

2. Sweet Interactions (NOT flee - as requested):
   - "No" button teleports randomly when hovered (up to 3 times)
   - After teleport limit, button stays for clicking
   - Click triggers sweet message with hearts & confetti

3. Romance Elements:
   - Vintage newspaper aesthetic with beige background
   - Sweet pink accents and loving typography
   - Floating hearts and confetti effects
   - Personal image container for your photo together

4. Technical Implementation:
   - Clean HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript (ES6 modules)
   - CSS variables for easy customization
   - Modular JavaScript architecture
   - Performance optimized animations
   - Mobile-responsive layout

HOW TO USE:
1. Open index.html in any modern browser
2. Click anywhere on the newspaper to flip it open
3. Try to click the "No" button - it will teleport around playfully
4. After a few attempts, click it to see the sweet message
5. Replace assets/images/placeholder-image.png with your photo
6. Customize the message in index.html:
   - Replace [Her Name] with your girlfriend's name
   - Replace [Your Name] with your name
   - Edit the heartfelt message as desired
